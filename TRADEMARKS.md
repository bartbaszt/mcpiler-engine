- State truthfully that your software uses, embeds, integrates with, or is compatible with

- Use the name in unmodified form to refer to this project, for example in documentation,

- Redistribute unmodified builds of MCPiler Engine under their original name.

- Name a fork, derivative, redistribution or competing product "MCPiler" or any name
  confusingly similar to it.
- Use the MCPiler name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation or official

  or service name that could cause confusion about origin.

If you fork this repository and publish modified builds, choose a different name and make
the relationship to the original clear and accurate.

Questions about permitted use: contact the maintainer.
